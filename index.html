06 / Common questions

Good to
know.

A few useful things to consider before you contact a contractor.

What type of projects can I enquire about?

KUZ publicly lists residential construction, commercial construction, interior fit-outs and maintenance. You can select any of these in the enquiry form.

Can I enquire before my plans are complete?

Yes, you can prepare an initial enquiry with what you know so far. Ask KUZ which drawings, approvals or other details will be needed before work can start.

How much will my construction project cost?

Cost depends on the design, size, site, materials and scope. Share your budget and request a project-specific quotation; no fixed construction rate is promised by this preview.

How long will the project take?

Timing varies with the scope, approvals, material availability and site conditions. Discuss your target start and completion dates with the contractor.

How can I share my drawings or photos?

Use the enquiry form to prepare a WhatsApp message. Attach drawings or reference photographs in WhatsApp after the chat opens.

Where is KUZ based?

The public business directory lists KUZ in Bluffhill, Harare. Confirm the current office address with the company before visiting.
